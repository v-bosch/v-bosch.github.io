I am a PhD student supervised by Prof. Dr. Tim C Kietzmann in the Machine Learning group at the Institute for Cognitive Science, University of Osnabrück. 

I work at the intersection of cognitive computational neuroscience and machine learning, in the field of [neuroconnectionism](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-023-00705-w). My research asks what neural representations encode, how that information is structured, and when representations align across biological and artificial systems. Currently, I work on these questions by developing neural network architectures that model vision, and the neural decoding of visual representations from brain activity. Beyond my experimental work, I am deeply interested in the theoretical and philosophical assumptions behind the methods used in NeuroAI, and how to interpret representational alignment between biological and artificial neural networks. I hope to extend this work to understand how structural representational formats may support robustness and generalisation, and under which constraints different systems may develop similar representations.
